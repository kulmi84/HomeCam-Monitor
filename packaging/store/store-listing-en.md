# HomeCam Monitor – English Store listing

## Short description

Keep your cameras in view: live video, local motion detection and recordings for Windows.

## Description

A compatible network camera of your own is required.

HomeCam Monitor displays your cameras in a compact Windows window or a grid with up to four cameras. Connect compatible ONVIF/RTSP cameras, camera streams from Home Assistant, or supported Reolink cameras using UID. Home Assistant is not required for direct network streams or Reolink UID connections.

Manage your cameras in one place, configure local motion detection, and save videos or snapshots to a recording folder of your choice. Supported motion and person events depend on the camera and connection method. Local motion detection does not classify people.

Connectivity depends on your camera, network and firewall. UID connections may require services provided by the camera manufacturer. Camera hardware is not included.

The app follows your Windows display language: German on German-language Windows, English for other display languages. Existing camera names and settings are preserved.

Support: https://github.com/kulmi84/HomeCam-Monitor/issues. Privacy and confidential enquiries: support@dorisundmarcin.de

## Features

- Live video in a single-camera window or a grid with up to four cameras
- Compatible ONVIF/RTSP streams, Home Assistant cameras and supported Reolink cameras using UID
- Local motion detection, video recordings and snapshots
- Camera and recording settings, with optional Windows startup
